Supportive
The palette should feel like a gentle hand on your shoulder — present, reassuring, never overwhelming.
Intentional
Every color choice should feel considered and purposeful — like meditation, not accident.
Calm
The visual language should slow breathing, not quicken it. Serenity over urgency.
Design Philosophy
Do
✓Use tonal layering for depth
✓Let whitespace breathe
✓Use IBM Plex Serif for headings
✓Keep Ira teal pure — no blue tones
✓Soft terracotta for errors (not red)
Don't
✗Use clinical red for errors
✗Add drop shadows to non-elevated elements
✗Mix Ira teal with blue-toned colors
✗Use urgency-language colors (amber, red) without care
✗Use more than 2 typefaces
